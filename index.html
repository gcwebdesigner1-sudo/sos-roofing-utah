Who we are

A roofer you won't have to chase down

Everybody has heard a roofer horror story. Cheap materials, corners cut, a deposit taken and then nobody answers the phone. We've spent more than 30 years being the opposite of that.

SOS Roofing isn't an out-of-state outfit following hailstorms around. We live and work in Roy, and we roof homes from North Salt Lake up to Brigham City. Steve and the crew show up when they say they will, protect your yard, and clean up so well you'd hardly know they were there.

  • One team oversees your whole project
  • Help with insurance claims from start to finish
  • Military discounts

How it works

Four steps.
Zero stress.

No pushy sales visit. Here's exactly what happens after you reach out.

1

Request an estimate

One of our roofers comes out, measures your roof and looks it over. You don't even need to be home.

2

Get it by email

You get a detailed written estimate in your inbox. Sign it digitally, send it back, and we'll call to set a date.

3

We do the work

Most replacements take about two days:

  • Old shingles come off
  • Plants, shrubs and decks get covered
  • New roof goes on, then a full cleanup
